How EcoLocate Works

Disposing of obsolete technology responsibly should be effortless. We streamline the journey in three clear steps.

01

Find Nearby Facilities

Filter verified recycling centres by State, City, or specific device categories like laptops, smartphones, batteries, and appliances.

02

Drop Off or Schedule

Check operating hours, accepted electronics, and contact details. Get turn-by-turn directions directly to the facility.

03

Earn EcoRewards

Estimate your positive environmental impact through our calculator and claim points for turning old tech into circular resources.

Why It Matters

Designed for Tangible Environmental Impact

Transforming how households and enterprises dispose of retired technology through transparency, compliance, and incentives.

Zero-Landfill Guarantee

All listed facilities adhere to strict e-waste handling protocols to prevent heavy metals from leaching into water supplies and agricultural soil.

Precious Metal Recovery

Recover vital copper, gold, palladium, and rare earth elements, significantly reducing energy-intensive mining operations worldwide.

Health Hazard Prevention

Avoid informal open-air burning and acid-bath dismantling that causes severe respiratory, neurological, and kidney damage.
