Our Mission

Bridging Society to Circular Technology

EcoLocate was created to solve the fundamental friction between consumers holding obsolete electronics and certified facilities equipped to recycle them safely.

Why EcoLocate Exists

Every year, millions of metric tons of electrical devices are tossed into general waste bins or stored indefinitely in drawers due to a lack of awareness about where and how to recycle them.

EcoLocate provides a unified digital directory of accredited e-waste facilities, demystifies hazardous toxins, and incentivizes responsible stewardship through our EcoRewards framework.

Action Pillars

How We Drive Behavioral Change

We believe that combining accessibility, transparent education, and community rewards is the key to achieving zero e-waste in our landfills.

Radical Accessibility

Locating an authorized collection facility should be as effortless as finding a grocery store. We catalogue verified facilities by state, city, and accepted device categories.

Demystifying Toxins

We translate chemical hazards (such as Lead, Mercury, and Cadmium) into clear, actionable advice so citizens understand the immediate danger of improper disposal.

EcoRewards Incentives

Recycling creates tangible economic value by recovering precious metals. We introduce positive reinforcement where eco-conscious actions yield community rewards and recognition.

Development Plan

EcoLocate Multi-Phase Architecture

Following software engineering best practices, EcoLocate is developed in structured phases:

Phase 1 • Complete

Frontend Architecture

HTML5, modern CSS3 styling, vanilla JavaScript directory filter & rewards calculator.

Phase 2 • Current

Python / Flask & MySQL

Secure user & admin authentication, database tables, sessions & CSRF protection.

Phase 3

Facility CSV Ingestion

Automated ingestion of nationwide authorized recycling facility registries.

Phase 4

Dynamic Backend Search

High-performance SQL indexing, filtering APIs & reward tracking audit logs.

Phase 5

Leaflet & Geospatial Maps

Interactive dynamic GIS maps, radius geocoding, and live GPS routing.
